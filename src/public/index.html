LAN Camera Streaming

Self-hosted low-latency camera streaming over local network via WebRTC

Choose Your Role

Stream a camera from a phone or laptop, or watch a stream from another device on the same local network.

📹 Start Streaming (Sender) 📺 Watch Stream (Viewer)

Quick Instructions

  1. Ensure both devices are on the same local Wi-Fi or connected via a secure HTTPS tunnel.
  2. On the camera device, click Start Streaming, enter the access PIN, and generate or enter a Room ID.
  3. Grant browser camera permission when prompted.
  4. On the receiving device, click Watch Stream, enter the same Room ID and access PIN.
  5. Direct WebRTC media connects peer-to-peer without server media relay.
